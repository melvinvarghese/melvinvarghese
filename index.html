A little context

A librarian's mind.
A maker's curiosity.

I'm a librarian by profession and a curious learner by nature.

I like collecting ideas, keeping notes, discovering stories, and making small digital things that solve interesting problems. My projects often begin with a simple question: Wouldn't it be useful if…?

Outside the library, you'll often find me reading, writing, watching films, exploring philosophical questions or tinkering with an idea that refuses to leave me alone.
