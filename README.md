## A little about me

I'm a librarian by profession and a curious learner by nature. I like collecting ideas, keeping notes, discovering stories, and making small digital things that solve interesting problems.

My projects often begin with a simple question: *Wouldn't it be useful if…?*

## Things I enjoy exploring

- **Reading and knowledge** — digital reading, commonplace books, annotations, and personal knowledge management.
- **Cinema and storytelling** — film diaries, film discovery, and the ways stories shape how we see the world.
- **Language and access** — Malayalam language resources and tools that make information easier to reach.
- **Creative technology** — small web apps, simulations, open-source experiments, and ideas made tangible.
