## What I’m interested in

- **Books and reading** — digital reading, personal knowledge management, and commonplace books.
- **Film and storytelling** — cinema, film diaries, and tools that help people explore what they watch.
- **Language and access** — Malayalam language resources and reader-friendly tools.
- **Creative technology** — small web applications, simulations, open-source software, and experiments built around interesting ideas.
